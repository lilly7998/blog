Tetris was written in 1984 by Alexey Pajitnov in Moscow. Forty-odd years later the rules still fit in one line: seven pieces fall, fill a whole row and it disappears; stack to the top and the game ends.

This version follows modern rules: a 7-bag randomizer (no endless droughts of the long I piece), a preview of the next three pieces, a hold slot, a ghost piece showing where it lands, and half a second of lock delay so you can still slide a piece after it touches down. Every 10 lines you level up and the pieces fall faster.

## How to play

**Phone:** six buttons at the bottom (hold, left, down, right, hard drop, rotate); left, right and down repeat when held. You can also swipe left or right to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop, swipe up to hold.

**Keyboard:** ←→ move, ↑ or X rotate, Z rotate back, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, C hold, P pause.

**Tip:** keep the rightmost column empty and wait for the I piece. Clearing four lines at once scores the most (800 × level).
