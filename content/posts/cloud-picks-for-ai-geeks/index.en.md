You don't need to spend money to give your AI agents a home, put up a small site, or run a model experiment. Below are well-regarded cloud resources with free tiers, grouped into six kinds by what they're for, each with what it is and how far the free plan goes.

## Cloud servers and VPS

{{< pick name="Oracle Cloud Always Free" url="https://www.oracle.com/cloud/free/" mark="" >}}The best-loved always-free cloud servers, with generous ARM instances; sign-up needs a card for verification, and popular regions are often out of capacity.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Google Cloud Free Tier" url="https://cloud.google.com/free" mark="" >}}One e2-micro VM free forever in selected US regions, plus trial credit for new users; a card is required.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="AWS Free Tier" url="https://aws.amazon.com/free/" mark="" >}}New accounts get trial credit to try EC2, S3, Lambda and more; a card is required, so set a billing alert.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Azure Free Account" url="https://azure.microsoft.com/free/" mark="" >}}Twelve months of popular services free for new accounts, plus first-month credit; a card is required.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="EUserv VS2-free" url="https://www.euserv.com/en/virtual-private-server/root-vserver/v2/vs2-free.php" mark="" >}}A free IPv6-only mini VPS from a long-running German host; tiny and often out of stock, fine for IPv6 experiments and light services.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Openworld" url="https://openworld.eu.org" mark="own" >}}A community-run free Proxmox VPS in Europe, renewed week by week; often out of stock, so you wait for restocks.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="weeny.cloud" url="https://weeny.cloud" mark="wait" >}}Lightweight VPS built for AI agents; free slots come through a waitlist.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Altare" url="https://altare.gg" mark="own" >}}Every team gets an always-free pool of 6 GB RAM, 1.5 cores and 10 GB disk, split across up to 4 servers, including Linux sandboxes with root SSH; no uptime guarantee.{{< /pick >}}


## Dev environments and notebooks

{{< pick name="InstaBox" url="https://instabox.sh" mark="own" >}}Cloud Linux machines for developers and agents; the free plan gives 4 cores and 8 GB, pauses when idle, and runs browser VS Code and Jupyter fine.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="GitHub Codespaces" url="https://github.com/features/codespaces" mark="" >}}VS Code dev containers in the browser, with free core-hours every month for personal accounts.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Google Colab" url="https://colab.research.google.com" mark="" >}}Free Jupyter notebooks with limited GPU access, the classic first stop for model experiments.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Kaggle Notebooks" url="https://www.kaggle.com" mark="" >}}Free weekly GPU/TPU hours, plus a deep library of datasets and competitions.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Lightning AI Studios" url="https://lightning.ai" mark="" >}}Cloud dev studios with ready-to-use GPU environments and a monthly free allowance.{{< /pick >}}


## App hosting

{{< pick name="GitHub Pages" url="https://pages.github.com" mark="own" >}}Free static site hosting; pairs with Actions for automatic builds and supports custom domains with HTTPS.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Cloudflare Workers & Pages" url="https://workers.cloudflare.com" mark="" >}}Run JS/TS on Cloudflare's global edge; the free plan allows 100,000 requests a day, with unlimited bandwidth for static sites.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Vercel" url="https://vercel.com" mark="" >}}The go-to for front ends and Next.js; the free Hobby plan is for non-commercial use.{{< /pick >}}

{{< pick name="Hugging Face Spaces" url="https://huggingface.co/spaces" mark="" >}}One-click Gradio, Streamlit or Docker demos on free CPU instances, the standard way to show off AI work.{{< /pick >}}
